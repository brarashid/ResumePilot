Our Mission

Levelling the playing field for every job seeker

In 2023, our founders were applying for jobs across three continents and kept hitting the same wall — strong candidates rejected by automated systems before any human ever read their resumes.

They realised that the rules of job applications had fundamentally changed, but the tools available to candidates hadn't kept up. ATS systems gate-keep over 75% of corporate applications, yet most candidates have no idea how to optimise for them.

ResumePilot was built to close that gap — using AI to analyse job descriptions, extract key requirements, and rewrite resumes to pass ATS filters in under 2 minutes.

Today, we help job seekers across 40+ countries compete on equal footing, regardless of where they went to school or who they know.

What We Stand For

Our values

Every product decision we make is guided by these six principles.

Equal Access

Career opportunities shouldn't depend on having the right connections or the budget for a professional resume writer. Great tools should be available to everyone, everywhere.

Speed & Simplicity

Your time is valuable. We obsess over removing unnecessary steps so you can go from job description to tailored resume in minutes, not hours.

Privacy First

Your career data is sensitive. We never sell it, never share it without explicit consent, and give you full control to export or delete it at any time.

Global by Default

Resume standards vary dramatically by region. We build for the world — not just Silicon Valley — with templates optimised for every major job market.

Evidence-Based

Every feature is grounded in research. We study how ATS systems actually work and what hiring managers actually look for, and update our models accordingly.

Honest Pricing

No dark patterns, no surprise charges. Our free tier is genuinely useful, and our Pro plan is priced to be accessible to job seekers at any income level.
