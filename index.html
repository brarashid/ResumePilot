FAQ

Common questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is ResumePilot?
ResumePilot is an AI-powered resume builder that analyses job descriptions, tailors your resume for ATS systems, generates cover letters, and tracks your applications — all in one platform.
What does ATS-friendly mean?
ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System — software that most companies use to automatically filter resumes before a human ever reads them. An ATS-friendly resume uses the right keywords, structure, and formatting to pass these filters. ResumePilot scores your resume from 0–100 and shows you exactly what to fix.
How does the AI work?
You paste a job description, and our AI extracts the key requirements, keywords, and skills the employer is looking for. It then cross-references your profile and rewrites your resume to perfectly match the role — without you having to do it manually for every application.
Is ResumePilot free?
Yes. You can create up to 3 AI-generated resumes, run ATS checks, and download as PDF or DOCX completely free. Upgrade to Pro ($9.99/mo) for unlimited resumes, cover letters, application tracking, and all 9 regional templates.
What file formats can I download?
You can download your resume as a PDF or a DOCX (Word) file. Both formats are ATS-compatible and accepted by all major job boards and applicant tracking systems.
How long does it take to build a resume?
Most users complete their first resume in under 10 minutes. Once your profile is set up, generating a tailored resume for a new job takes less than 2 minutes — just paste the job description and click Generate.
Which regions and countries are supported?
ResumePilot has templates and formatting optimised for US/Canada, Europe (including Europass-compatible formats), Africa, and the Gulf/MENA region. Resume standards vary significantly between regions, and we account for all of them.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Your profile and resume data are stored securely and never shared with third parties or employers without your explicit action. You can export or delete your data at any time from your account settings.
What's the difference between a resume and a CV?
A resume is a concise 1–2 page document focused on work experience and skills, common in the US and Canada. A CV (curriculum vitae) is a longer, more detailed document that includes academic credentials, publications, and more — standard in Europe, Africa, and parts of the Middle East. ResumePilot supports both formats based on your target region.
